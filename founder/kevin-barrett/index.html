Why iT Factor Exists

Why Kevin built iT Factor

After seeing performers lose time and confidence to fragmented tools, technical failures, inconsistent exports, and workflows that were not designed around real auditions, Kevin began developing the studio he believed actors should already have.

A self-tape can carry real career weight, yet actors are often asked to move between camera tools, project notes, slates, editors, file utilities, and delivery systems while staying focused on the performance. Kevin saw the need for one workflow that treated the audition as a connected process.

Actors deserve a professional workflow that supports the performance instead of competing with it.

That idea became iT Factor: recording, project organization, take review, slates, editing, file preparation, and delivery brought together in one actor-focused studio.

Product Decisions in Practice

From the casting room to the actor’s iPhone

As founder and product lead, Kevin translates real audition-workflow needs into the priorities that shape iT Factor.

Prepare

Projects and slates stay connected.

Organizing audition details and preparing slates in the same workflow reduces the number of moving pieces before recording starts.

Perform

Recording protects the actor’s focus.

A clear recording flow is designed to keep technical setup from taking over the work happening in front of the camera.

Review

Takes can be compared with intention.

Project-based review and ratings make it easier to assess performances without searching through a general camera roll.

Deliver

Editing, file preparation, and export belong together.

Trimming, naming, preparing, and exporting in one system helps the final file arrive with less avoidable friction.
