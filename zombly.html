An independent compatibility utility

Bring compatible legacy Yummly cooking probes back to your iPhone.

Zombly is an independent iPhone utility designed to scan for, connect to, and display candidate readings from compatible legacy Yummly Bluetooth cooking thermometers and probes. Scan, connect, and check basic compatibility for free.

How it works

A local compatibility path from scan to Cook mode.

Scan locally.

Zombly uses Bluetooth on the iPhone to find nearby probes. It does not require a Zombly account or cloud service.

Check compatibility for free.

Connect to a compatible-looking probe and check for a basic candidate temperature reading before starting Cook mode.

Cook with clear purchase terms.

Cook mode includes live monitoring, a timer, and target alerts. Zombly Lifetime Unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If the proposed 3-day trial is offered, it does not renew and does not automatically charge the user.

Privacy

Probe activity stays on the iPhone in the audited V1 app.

The audited Zombly V1 app keeps Bluetooth discovery and probe-reading data on the iPhone. It does not include an app account, Zombly backend, app analytics, ads, or third-party tracking. Apple processes App Store purchases through StoreKit. Read the full Zombly Privacy Policy for details.

Compatibility

Built for select legacy hardware, without a compatibility guarantee.

Zombly is intended for compatible legacy Yummly Bluetooth cooking thermometers and probes. Compatibility is not guaranteed for every model, firmware version, battery or hardware condition, Bluetooth environment, iPhone, iOS version, or future operating-system release.

Independent status: Zombly is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or supported by Yummly or any of Yummly's former or current owners. It is not an official replacement app, warranty service, repair provider, or manufacturer support channel.

Safety

Use a calibrated thermometer as the safety check.

Zombly displays candidate temperature data decoded from Bluetooth probe readings. Food-safety decisions remain the user's responsibility. Always verify food safety with a properly calibrated food thermometer, follow applicable food-handling guidance, and do not rely on Zombly as the only safety check.
